## Scope

For each admitted/scorable weekly run, calculate and persist results for all six §12.3 benchmarks and both variants. The benchmark identities are SPY; exposure-matched SPY; equal-weight universe; sector-ETF-matched; quant baseline book; random committee. `ADJUSTED` applies the run's kill switch from its halt mark, transferring affected exposure to T-bills; `UNADJUSTED` ignores the switch. The adjusted series is primary and both are retained. Each weekly period uses the run's entry reference and its immutable same-run `benchmark_period_references` at the following scheduled weekly execution session (normally next Monday; calendar shifts a closed Monday), with the same P6.5 ticket cutoff as the strategy result. Never borrow another run's endpoint. Use the existing P6.5 scorer admission and outcome path as the source of scorable scope and result cutoffs.

For each tradable instrument, calculate Abdi–Ranaldo and Corwin–Schultz independently from that instrument's own point-in-time eligible SIP daily bars. Floor negative daily estimates at zero before forming each estimator's 21-session effective-spread estimate, then calculate `spread_est = 0.5 * spread_AR_21d + 0.5 * spread_CS_21d`. Both components are required; if either is missing or invalid for a required instrument/date, cost is `UNRESOLVED` and the benchmark result fails closed. Per-side modeled cost is `0.5 * spread_est + 5 bps`, charged on that instrument's traded notional derived from `Σ|w_target − w_drifted_prev|`. Never reuse one instrument's spread for another. Cash and T-bill legs have no cost. This equal-weight combination is a design choice and makes no claim that the literature establishes it as optimal. Random committee is exactly K=1000 deterministic joint permutations of whole per-name verdict bundles, seeded from the durable commitment hash; apply the normal sector, volatility, and portfolio sizing constraints to each target name. Persist sufficient inputs, identity/seed, variant, cost evidence, and outputs for exact replay. Missing scope, references, point-in-time bars/outcomes, benchmark inputs, or either cost estimate fails closed.

No P6.7 metrics/DSR/MinTRL/CSCV, P6.8 robustness, P6.9 ablations, P6.10 probes, P6.11 reporting, or `gate-P6` work.

## Boundary checks

- Pure benchmark math must not import providers, SQLAlchemy, or store modules; all data access is behind ticket-gated orchestration adapters and `store/as_of.py`.
- The new evaluator consumes immutable entry and same-run period-end references plus P6.5 cutoff-bounded outcome evidence; it cannot borrow another run's endpoint or make a second, broader eligibility decision.
- Costs are attributed to instrument, estimator, eligible 21-session input window, both component estimates, equal-weight combined spread, turnover, and per-side rate. A missing or invalid Abdi–Ranaldo or Corwin–Schultz estimate makes that instrument's cost unresolved; cash and T-bill legs incur no instrument cost.
- Benchmark and cost records are append-only, with a durable deterministic replay key; exact duplicate replay is idempotent and conflicting replay fails closed.
- Random committee moves whole verdict bundles as a single permutation, never changes identity in any persisted prompt, performs no LLM/CIO work, and applies existing sizing constraints.
- Generate contract artifacts through `contracts.schema_export`; do not hand-edit generated schemas. Preserve all existing dirty files and their contents.

## Owner decisions

Resolved by owner 2026-09-27: compute Abdi–Ranaldo and Corwin–Schultz independently from each instrument's own SIP daily bars; floor each estimator's negative daily values before forming its 21-session estimate; average the two component estimates equally; require both or fail closed; charge half the combined spread plus 5 bps per side to each tradable instrument; do not charge cash/T-bill legs. The design rationale is recorded in blueprint §5/§9: retain both distinct low-frequency estimators without claiming one is superior, reduce estimator-specific measurement noise by equal weighting, and make no literature claim that the mean is optimal. Other benchmark identities, adjusted/unadjusted semantics, K, seed source, and period boundaries are pinned in blueprint §12.3 and §9.
