## Resolved pins (P6.0)

Full text is in the blueprint (§4.6, §9, §12.1-§12.6). Summary:

- **D9 aGRAPA (verified from the primary paper, arXiv 2010.09686 App. B.3, eq. 26).** `μ̂_t = (1/2 + Σ X_i)/(t+1)`, `σ̂²_t = (1/4 + Σ (X_i − μ̂_i)²)/(t+1)`, `λ_t = clip((μ̂_{t−1} − m)/(σ̂²_{t−1} + (μ̂_{t−1} − m)²), −c/(1−m), c/m)`, `μ̂_0 = 1/2`, `σ̂²_0 = 1/4`, `c ≤ 1`. Here `X = (y+1)/2`, `m = 1/2`, one-sided (λ ≥ 0), `c = 1/2`: `λ_t = clip(μ̂ᵧ/(σ̂²ᵧ + μ̂ᵧ²), 0, 1/2)` in `y = x/c_x` units with `μ̂ᵧ,0 = 0`, `σ̂²ᵧ,0 = 1`, **`λ_1 = 0`**. Numerically checked (paper form and `y` form give identical λ/2 and wealth to 1e-15). `confseq` 0.0.11 was read as a cross-check only: it uses `trunc_scale = 1/2` and does not implement aGRAPA, so there is no disagreement with the paper. My earlier recollection of a `t+2` denominator was wrong; the paper says `t+1`.
- **D12 FAIL(a)/FAIL(b) (revised 2026-09-26 after a primary-source review; supersedes the TOST decision below).** FAIL(b) is removed from the decision rule: the fixed-b TOST's one-sided components reject 5.8-6.7% at the AR(0.2)/AR(0.3)/skewed boundary nulls, and no reviewed literature gives a valid dependence-robust equivalence test at T = 104 with usable power for a ±0.02/year margin (known-variance TOST power at T = 104: 0.76 at 1% tracking error, 0 at 2/5/10/15%). TOST/HAC stays as a diagnostic that never sets an outcome. FAIL is only the mirrored quant e-process ≥ 20 at an observed week t ≥ 26; the terminal rule (no terminal before 26, current wealth decides, first terminal week absorbing, same-week PASS+FAIL is INCONCLUSIVE `conflicting_evidence`, no crossing by 104 is INCONCLUSIVE) is pinned in blueprint §12.6. Original D12 text, kept for the diagnostic: **TOST/HAC (ratified).** α = 0.05 once at observed week 104; margin ±0.02/52 arithmetic weekly mean; Bartlett HAC with `L = ⌊4(T/100)^{2/9}⌋` (= 4) taken as Kiefer-Vogelsang's `M` (weights `1 − j/L`), `b = L/T`. Critical value = Kiefer & Vogelsang (2005) Table I, 95% right tail, linearly interpolated: `cv(4/104) = 1.7278` (table values 1.690 at b = 0.02, 1.731 at 0.04). The paper gives a table, not a closed formula. Brownian-functional simulation (1.711, discretization ~0.01 low) and the earlier 1.736 scratch value are consistent; `(L+1)/T` (1.748) is a reported sensitivity. Simulation is validation, not calibration.
- **Observed-week counting (26/104).** Observed evaluation periods, not calendar weeks; `MISSING` weeks are skipped and listed; an `UNRESOLVED` halted week blocks the test.
- **D10.** Hodrick removed. 5-day non-overlapping IC t is the only claim-bearing signal statistic; 21/63-day ICs descriptive with every deterministic offset (5 and 13), no median promoted.
- **Eligible historical trade (D3).** Latest eligible SIP trade at or before Monday open + 30 min; regular session; every condition must be last-sale eligible under the CTS (tapes A/B) / UTP (tape C) rules Alpaca documents, keyed by `(tape, code)`; unknown code on the candidate makes the reference unresolved; entitlement failure fails closed; allowlist finalized against the CTS/UTP specs and a recorded `/v2/stocks/meta/conditions/trade` response.
- **Date transplant (ratified, statistic aligned to the target).** Arms `T`, `N`, `S` (−72 months, month-preserving), placebo `P` (+7 days); item = (security, week, agent), cell = (model, quarter); `Δ = logit p(S) − logit p(T)` vs `x = y(d′) − y(d)` where `y` is the security's sector-relative 5-day outcome (not SPY); Spearman `ρ`; week-clustered permutation (10,000, seed from `sha256(cell id)`); BH q = 0.05 per model across all probes; minimum 12 weeks and 200 items; no effect-size threshold; effect size and interval reported. Flag = BH-adjusted `p_S ≤ q` AND `ρ_S > ρ_P` AND placebo not significant. Adapted from HindsightBench (arXiv 2607.18867); the decision rule is ours.
- **D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, D6, D7, D8, D13, D14, D15/16, D17, D18** as approved by the owner (halt classes, weekly reference chain, SIP-trade reference, halt marks, per-instrument costs, delisting default flagged, K = 1000 joint permutation, DGS3MO vintages, trial identity, anchor ordering, ablation and probe scope in P6, same-week absorbing PASS, all offsets).
